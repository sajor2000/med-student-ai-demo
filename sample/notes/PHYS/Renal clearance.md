# Renal clearance

Source: `Renal clearance.pdf` (slides)

## [S1] Renal clearance (sample lecture)
- Synthetic demo lecture for med-student-ai-demo
- Learning objectives: (1) define clearance; (2) estimate GFR; (3) interpret clearance relative to inulin

## [S2] Definition of clearance
- Clearance = (U x V) / P: the plasma volume cleared of a substance per unit time
- U = urine concentration, V = urine flow rate, P = plasma concentration
- Units are mL/min

## [S3] Inulin and GFR
- Inulin is freely filtered and neither reabsorbed nor secreted
- So inulin clearance equals GFR (about 125 mL/min in a healthy adult)
- Creatinine clearance approximates GFR but slightly overestimates it (small tubular secretion)

## [S4] Interpreting clearance
- Clearance below GFR: net reabsorption (for example glucose, which is 0 at normal plasma levels)
- Clearance above GFR: net secretion (for example para-aminohippuric acid, PAH)
- PAH clearance approximates renal plasma flow (about 600 mL/min)
