## Mechanism chain: exposure to treatment
- **Exposure:** a wrestler who shares mats and towels, with teammates who also had "boils". [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P22]] [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P18]] CA-MRSA skin abscesses cluster in healthy young people with skin contact. [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P120]] [PMID:22759601]
- **Recognition:** TLR2 senses bacterial lipoproteins; macrophages release IL-1, TNF-α, and IL-8. [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P55]] [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P56]] [PMID:18406368]
- **Recruitment:** rolling (E/P-selectin), activation (IL-8), tight adhesion (ICAM-1 with LFA-1/Mac-1), transmigration (PECAM-1). [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#T5]]
- **Evasion:** protein A binds IgG Fc and blocks opsonization; coagulase builds a fibrin wall; PVL kills leukocytes. [[MICRO/Staphylococcus aureus (sample lecture)#S3]] [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#T6]] [PMID:17146447]
- **Killing:** NADPH oxidase makes superoxide, myeloperoxidase makes hypochlorite. [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P60]] CGD is the failure case. [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P63]] [PMID:11997027]
- **Lab identity:** gram-positive cocci in clusters, catalase and coagulase positive, cefoxitin-resistant means mecA/PBP2a (MRSA). [[MICRO/Staphylococcus aureus (sample lecture)#S2]] [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#T4]] [[MICRO/Staphylococcus aureus (sample lecture)#S6]]
- **Treatment:** incision and drainage first; fever plus tachycardia (SIRS) adds an MRSA-active antibiotic, and TMP-SMX raised cure after drainage (80.5% vs 73.6%). [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#P157]] [PMID:24973422] [PMID:26962903]

## Beyond the slides
- Exfoliative toxin cleaves desmoglein-1 in the superficial epidermis (scalded skin). [PMID:11062541]
- Pitfall: a dihydrorhodamine (DHR) test can look "CGD-like" in myeloperoxidase deficiency, so the guide's CGD question uses an NBT test instead. [PMID:27301573]
- Catalase-positive organisms are classically blamed in CGD, but catalase-negative *S. aureus* were equally virulent in a mouse model. [PMID:11997027]

1. E-selectin (and P-selectin) for rolling; ICAM-1 binding integrins for tight adhesion. [[GUIDE/BMS 501 Self-Study Guide 02 Jordan Rivera (fictional sample)#T5]]
