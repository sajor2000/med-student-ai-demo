Beyond the slides

  • Exfoliative toxin cleaves desmoglein-1 in the superficial epidermis (scalded skin). PMID 11062541
  • Pitfall: a dihydrorhodamine (DHR) test can look "CGD-like" in myeloperoxidase deficiency, so the guide's CGD question uses an NBT test instead. PMID 27301573
  • Catalase-positive organisms are classically blamed in CGD, but catalase-negative S. aureus were equally virulent in a mouse model. PMID 11997027
